# Security Policy

ReviewVow takes security reports seriously. Thank you for helping keep our users and systems safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@reviewvow.com** with:

- a description of the issue and its impact
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- the affected repository, URL or component

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

You can also see our contact details at <https://reviewvow.com/.well-known/security.txt>.

## What to expect

We aim to acknowledge reports within a few business days and to keep you updated until the issue is resolved. ReviewVow is a small team, so these are goals rather than guarantees.

## Scope

This policy covers the repositories in the `reviewvow` GitHub organization and the `reviewvow.com` website.

## Good-faith research

Please avoid accessing or modifying data that is not yours, degrading service, or testing against other people's accounts. If you follow these guidelines, we will not pursue action against you for good-faith research.
